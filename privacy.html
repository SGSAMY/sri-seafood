Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Sri Seafood (“we”, “us”) is a sole-trader food business based in Royal Tunbridge Wells, Kent. This notice explains what personal information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, postcode and message.
  • Orders: the details you send us on WhatsApp — your name, delivery address or postcode, phone number and the items you order.
  • Online payments (if enabled): processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Your basket is saved only in your own browser so it's there when you return.

How we use it

Only to respond to enquiries, process and deliver your orders, and — if you join our WhatsApp community — to share fresh lists and offers. We don't sell your information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the business: Netlify (website hosting and forms), WhatsApp/Meta (order messages), and Stripe (card payments, if enabled).

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes; enquiries are deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Email info@sriseafood.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
