Delivery & questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery and keeping your seafood at its best.

How do I order?
Add what you'd like to your basket and tap Send order on WhatsApp — your order arrives with us as a ready-written message. You can also message us directly on +44 7733 689 686 or join our WhatsApp community to see each drop's fresh list.
When do you deliver?
Our regular fresh drop is every Saturday. Tuesday deliveries are subject to availability — when we have a Tuesday slot we share it first in the WhatsApp community, and you can always ask when you order.
How much is delivery?
Delivery is free on orders over £45. For smaller orders we'll confirm any delivery charge when we confirm your order.
Where do you deliver?
We deliver across Kent, Sussex, Croydon and Orpington. Outside the area? Contact us with your postcode for a personalised quote.
Why are prices per kilo?
Fresh fish varies naturally in size. Prices are shown per kg, and we confirm the final weight and total with you before delivery, so there are no surprises.
What cuts can I choose?
Depending on the fish: Whole (unprepared whole fish), Cleaned (scaled, gutted and rinsed), Curry Cut (bone-in chunks) or Steak (thick cross-section slices). Prices differ by preparation and are shown on each product. Head and tail are included by default — just add a note if you'd rather not have them.
Is there a minimum order or a cut-off time?
The minimum is 500g per item, and orders close at 7pm the day before delivery.
How can I pay?
By card, cash or bank transfer. We check every item before it leaves us.
How is it packed?
Everything is vacuum-packed and delivered in an insulated cool box with ice, so it arrives fresh and cold.
How should I store my seafood?
Refrigerate as soon as it arrives (0–4°C) and enjoy within 1–2 days, or freeze on the day of delivery. Defrost in the fridge, never at room temperature.
Do you supply restaurants and businesses?
Yes — we're happy to discuss regular supply for restaurants, caterers and events. Get in touch.
